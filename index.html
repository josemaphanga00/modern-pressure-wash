Help & Advice

FAQ

Will pressure washing damage my surfaces?

Every property is assessed before work begins. Different materials require different restoration methods, which is why we carefully choose the safest approach for every surface. Protecting your property is always our priority.

How long does the restoration take?

Most residential projects are completed within a day, although larger or more complex properties may require additional time. We'll always discuss the expected timeframe with you before we begin.

Do I need to be home?

Not necessarily. As long as we have access to the agreed work areas and water supply, we can often complete the restoration while you're away.

How often should my property be restored?

That depends on your property's environment, surrounding vegetation and exposure to weather. Many homeowners choose our maintenance programme to keep their property looking its best throughout the year.

Do you provide free quotations?

Yes. Every quotation is free, with no obligation. We'll assess your property and recommend the most appropriate restoration plan.
