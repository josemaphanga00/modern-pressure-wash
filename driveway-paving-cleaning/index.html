Frequently Asked Questions

Questions Before You Book?

How much does driveway and paving cleaning cost?

Pricing depends on the area size, paving type, level of buildup, access and any specialist treatment required. We provide a free quotation after assessing the scope, often from photos and measurements.

Can pressure washing damage paving?

It can if the wrong pressure or technique is used. We assess the material and condition first, then choose an appropriate pressure-washing method for the surface.

Do you remove algae and organic buildup from paving?

Yes. Organic buildup such as algae and mould can be treated as part of the cleaning process, depending on the surface and severity of the contamination.

Do you clean large driveways and paved areas?

Yes. We clean residential driveways, estates, complexes, shared paved areas and larger commercial hard-surface areas. Larger scopes can be assessed from photos, measurements or a site visit.

Can I get a quotation from photos?

In many cases, yes. Clear photos, approximate measurements and a short description of the areas to be cleaned can be enough to prepare an initial quotation. A site assessment may still be required for larger or more complex work.
